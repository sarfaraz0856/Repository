TITLE
How OTP Really Works | Where the 6-Digit Code Comes From – OTP Explained

DESCRIPTION
How does an OTP work? 🔐 You pay online or log in, a 6-digit code arrives on your phone, and the moment you enter it the payment is confirmed – but where is that code made, how does it reach your phone, and how does the bank know it's right? In this animated explainer: how the server creates a random OTP, what a hash is, how the SMS gateway and telecom network deliver it, how the bank verifies it, how authenticator-app codes (TOTP) work offline – and how to stay safe from OTP fraud.

In this video you will learn:
✅ What an OTP is – one-time passwords and two-factor authentication
✅ The main parts – app, auth server, SMS gateway, telecom operator, phone
✅ How an OTP is created – random numbers, one million combinations, hashing, expiry
✅ How it reaches your phone – SMS center, sender ID, autofill
✅ How the bank checks it – expiry, limited attempts, single use
✅ How authenticator apps (TOTP) work
✅ How OTP fraud happens – fake calls, fake links, SIM swap, screen-share apps
✅ OTP safety tips (India Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930)
✅ The future: passkeys and biometric login
