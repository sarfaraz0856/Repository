ENGLISH DUB PACK – OTP SHORT (Hinglish)
1) Short upload hone ke baad: YouTube Studio → Content → Shorts → is Short par click → Languages.
2) "Add language" → English.
3) Dub → Add → how-otp-works-shorts-english-dub-audio.wav upload karein (length video ke barabar 47.3 sec hai).
4) Title & description → how-otp-works-shorts-english-title-description.txt se English title/description paste karein.
5) Subtitles → how-otp-works-shorts-english-subtitles.srt ("With timing").
Note: Agar aapke channel par Dub option nahi dikh raha, to pehle Full Video par jaise kiya tha waise hi check karein (feature har channel par ek saath nahi aata).
