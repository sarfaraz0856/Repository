Namaste doston! Pichhle do video mein humne home row aur saari keys ki finger position seekhi. Aaj hum typing test denge, aur dekhenge ki fifty WPM tak kaise pahunchein.
Sabse pehle samjho WPM kya hai. WPM matlab words per minute, yaani ek minute mein aap kitne shabd type karte ho. Typing test mein paanch letters ko ek word maana jaata hai.
Test dene ke liye typingtest dot com ya monkeytype dot com kholo, aur one minute ka test chuno. Ungliyan home row pe rakho. Pehla letter dabate hi timer shuru ho jaata hai.
Test ke time screen pe dekho, keyboard pe nahi. Agar galti ho jaaye, to ruko mat. Ek galti ke liye poora word mat mitao, bas aage badhte raho.
Ek minute baad result aayega. Do cheezein dekho: WPM aur accuracy. Accuracy kam se kam ninety five percent honi chahiye. Accuracy kam hai, to speed badhane ka koi fayda nahi.
Beginners aksar twenty se thirty WPM se shuru karte hain. Forty WPM achhi speed hai, aur fifty WPM se upar aap kaafi fast ho.
Fifty WPM tak pahunchne ka simple plan: roz das minute practice, aur din mein do test. Har hafte apna best score note karo. Har hafte paanch WPM bhi badhe, to ek mahine mein bada farak dikhega.
Aur ek tip: the, and, is jaise chhote common words ki practice zyada karo, kyunki ye baar baar aate hain.
