FASTag documentary – fact notes (for the creator)

The video explains the general working of FASTag / NETC in simplified form. Key points used:
- FASTag: passive RFID (UHF band) sticker on the windshield, linked to a prepaid wallet or bank account.
- NETC (National Electronic Toll Collection) programme, developed by NPCI.
- Roles: tag, lane RFID reader/antenna, toll plaza system, acquirer bank (toll plaza side), NETC switch (NPCI), issuer bank (tag holder side).
- FASTag made mandatory for most vehicles from February 2021.
- NHAI "One Vehicle One FASTag" policy: one active FASTag per vehicle.
- Common reasons for tag issues: low balance, blacklist, incomplete KYC, vehicle detail mismatch, damaged/misplaced tag.
- NHAI highway helpline: 1033.
- GNSS / satellite-based tolling: being worked on / piloted.

Verify before publishing / when rules change:
- Rules about the toll charged without a valid FASTag change from time to time – the video does NOT state a multiplier and asks viewers to check official rules.
- Check the latest on NHAI (nhai.gov.in), NPCI NETC (npci.org.in) and your issuer bank's website.
- All amounts, toll rates, names (Demo Bank, DEMO 1234), tag IDs and SMS texts in the video are illustrative.
