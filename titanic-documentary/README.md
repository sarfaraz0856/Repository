# Titanic 1912 – Hindi documentary (in production)

The next film for Itihaas Ke Saaye, made the same way as the Chernobyl, Bhopal and Fukushima films: 3D animation, captions, a synthesised score, Hindi narration in the cloned voice, and an English dub.

- [`script/Titanic-Script-Hindi.md`](script/Titanic-Script-Hindi.md) holds the cold open and the 10 chapters: the shots, the Hindi narration, the on-screen captions, the facts, and the list of 3D sets to build.
