2. DESCRIPTION  (English)
--------------
The full story of the Chernobyl nuclear disaster: the night Reactor 4 exploded on April 26, 1986, the firefighters who never came home, and the ghost city of Pripyat that was emptied forever.

At 1:23 a.m., a safety test began at Unit 4 of the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant. Seconds later, the emergency button AZ-5 was pressed, but a design flaw in the RBMK reactor made the power surge instead of falling. This documentary follows what really happened that night, and how the world has lived with it for nearly 40 years.

▶ In this video
• How Chernobyl happened: the safety test, an unstable reactor at low power, and the AZ-5 button
• The firefighters who faced the fire with no protection
• How the world found out: alarms at Sweden's Forsmark plant and a 20-second Soviet TV report
• The bio-robots: when machines failed, men were sent onto the roof for 90 seconds each
• Hospital No. 6 in Moscow, the zinc coffins and Mitino Cemetery
• Pripyat: a young city of 50,000 that emptied in a single day and never filled again
• The liquidators, the Red Forest, the Sarcophagus and the New Safe Confinement
• The trenches dug in the Red Forest in 2022, and the lesson of Chernobyl
