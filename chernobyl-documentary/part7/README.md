## Historical notes

- **The exclusion zone** covers about 2,600 km² of northern Ukraine and is entered through guarded checkpoints, such as Dytyatky on the road from Kyiv.
- **Looting:** through the 1990s, almost everything of value in Pripyat was carried off: furniture, fittings, metal and even radiators. Much of it was contaminated.
- **Nature:** with nobody living there, birch, poplar and pine spread across the city's lawns, pavements and roads.
- **Duga** (the "Russian Woodpecker") was a Soviet over-the-horizon early-warning radar at the Chernobyl-2 military site, a few kilometres from the plant. It stopped operating around 1989, and its huge antenna array still stands in the forest.
