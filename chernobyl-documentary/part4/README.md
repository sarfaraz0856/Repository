## Historical notes

- **The news:** on the evening of 28 April 1986, the Vremya news programme on Soviet television read a short statement of about 20 seconds. This came after Sweden had detected the fallout and asked for an explanation.
- **May Day:** the 1 May parades went ahead as usual, including in Kyiv, about 100 km from the plant, while the reactor was still releasing radioactive material.
- **The basement:** in early May, Oleksiy Ananenko, Valeriy Bezpalov and Borys Baranov waded into the flooded rooms under the reactor and opened the valves that let the water drain away. This prevented a possible steam explosion if the molten core reached the water.
- **The zone:** by early May the 30 km exclusion zone had been set up, and about 116,000 people had been evacuated from it in 1986.
- **The fire:** the burning graphite in the core was out by about 10 May 1986. By then the release had already spread over much of Europe.
