## Historical notes

- **The samosely** ("self-settlers") were mostly elderly villagers who went back to their homes inside the zone in the years after the evacuation. The authorities eventually tolerated them, and some lived out their lives there.
- **Thyroid cancer:** radioactive iodine in milk and food led to a sharp rise in thyroid cancer among people who were children or adolescents in 1986, mainly in Belarus, Ukraine and western Russia. By the mid-2000s several thousand cases had been diagnosed.
- **The 1991 fire:** on 11 October 1991 a fire broke out in the turbine hall of Unit 2 and part of the roof collapsed. The unit was never restarted.
- **The end of the USSR:** the Soviet Union was dissolved in December 1991, and the plant and the zone passed to independent Ukraine.
