# CHERNOBYL — 26 April 1986 (cinematic documentary short)

> **Full film so far:** [`Chernobyl_1986_Full_Film.mp4`](Chernobyl_1986_Full_Film.mp4) (2:55). It plays Part I (the night, below), then *The Morning After* ([`part2/`](part2/)) and *The Liquidators* ([`part3/`](part3/)) back to back. This is the start of a planned 12–13 minute documentary. `Chernobyl_1986_Hindi_Narration.mp4` is the earlier 1:55 cut with Hindi narration.

**File:** `Chernobyl_26_April_1986_Documentary.mp4`
**Format:** 1920×1080 (16:9), 24 fps, 55 s, H.264 High + AAC 320 kbps, 48 kHz stereo.

Every frame was rendered from a 3D scene built for this film (Three.js / WebGL in headless Chromium, with ray-marched volumetric smoke and fire). All sound was synthesised in code. The film contains no stock footage, no AI-generated video and no copyrighted music.

## Historical choices

- **Layout:** the units stand in a line (1–4), with the long turbine hall beside them. The red-and-white lattice vent stack stands between units 3 and 4. The explosion destroys unit 4's central hall, the east side of the block, and roofs that then catch fire.
- **Cooling towers:** units 1–4 were cooled by a pond, not towers. The hyperbolic towers in the opening aerial are the **unfinished towers of units 5 and 6**, which were under construction in 1986, with their cranes.
- **The explosion:** a steam explosion followed seconds later by a larger blast that threw out burning graphite and fuel. There is no mushroom cloud. The shot shows a flash, fire, debris and a rising column, with the sound arriving after the light.
- **Emergency lights:** Soviet fire engines carried **blue** rotating beacons. The red sweeping light comes from alarm beacons on the plant buildings, which are plausible but not documented.
- **Firefighters:** canvas coats, crested helmets with neck flaps, rubber boots, and some carrying oxygen breathing sets. The first crews had no radiation protection.
- **The night sky:** the moon was two days past full (there was a lunar eclipse on 24 April 1986), so a low moon glows behind the cloud.
- **The Ferris wheel:** the amusement park was due to open on 1 May 1986, and the wheel was never used.
- **No signage or lettering** appears in the film. The only text is the three titles.

## If you want photoreal footage later

The film is a CGI reconstruction. The vidIQ AI-video account available during production had only 9 credits, which wasn't enough to generate clips. If you get credits on any text-to-video model (Veo 3.x, Kling, Sora, Runway, Hailuo), you can generate replacement clips at the same durations and drop them into the same edit.
