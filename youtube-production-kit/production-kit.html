How To Use This Kit

This kit turns the written plan into an actual video. Work through it in four stages. Each timeline segment below gives you four things: the voice-over (read it aloud or feed it to the AI voice file included with this kit), the on-screen captions (type these as text overlays), the shot list (exactly what to screen-record on your phone), and editing notes (zooms, highlights, transitions).

The 4-stage workflow

  1. Record the voice-over — Use the AI Hindi voice file supplied with this kit, or record your own voice reading the red voice-over boxes. This becomes your master audio track and sets the final timing.
  2. Screen-record your phone — Follow the green shot lists. On Android, use the built-in Screen Recorder (swipe down → Screen record) at 1080p/30fps. Record each step slowly and cleanly. Use a demo Google account, never your private one.
  3. Edit & assemble — In any editor (CapCut, VN, KineMaster, or DaVinci Resolve on PC — all free), lay the voice-over first, then drop screen clips under it, add caption overlays, zoom-ins and highlights per the notes.
  4. Export & upload — Use the export settings on the last page, then upload with the SEO title, description and tags provided.
