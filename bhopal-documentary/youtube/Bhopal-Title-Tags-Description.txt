DESCRIPTION
-----------
On the night of 2–3 December 1984, about 40 tonnes of methyl isocyanate (MIC) gas leaked from Union Carbide's pesticide plant in Bhopal, India. The city was asleep.

This 15-minute documentary, narrated in Hindi, tells the whole story:
- what went wrong inside the plant
- the escape through the poisoned lanes
- the railway station where Ghulam Dastagir saved a trainload of passengers
- the hospitals, the morning after, and the toll
- Warren Anderson's arrest and release
- the 1989 settlement and the 2010 verdict
- the poison that is still in the ground
- the survivors' fight, which continues four decades later

The Madhya Pradesh government's official death toll is 3,787. Other estimates put the deaths in the first weeks at more than 8,000. More than 500,000 people were affected. Bhopal is still remembered as the world's worst industrial disaster.

A cinematic historical reconstruction (CGI). The narration is in Hindi, and English subtitles are available.
