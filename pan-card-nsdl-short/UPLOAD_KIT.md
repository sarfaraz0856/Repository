## Description
```
Apply for a PAN card online yourself on the NSDL / Protean portal — no agent, no middleman 🪪

⚠️ BIG UPDATE: from 1 April 2026, Form 49A is replaced.
Indian citizens → Form 93 | Company / firm / HUF → Form 94 | Foreign applicants → Form 95 / 96

STEP 1 — Open the official site: onlineservices.proteantech.in
STEP 2 — Choose "Apply for new PAN" → Form 93
STEP 3 — Fill name, DOB, email, mobile EXACTLY as on Aadhaar → save your token number
STEP 4 — Select Aadhaar e-KYC + e-Sign (100% paperless, no courier)
STEP 5 — Pay: ~₹66 e-PAN only / ~₹101 e-PAN + physical card (Indian address)
STEP 6 — Aadhaar OTP → e-Sign → save the 15-digit acknowledgement number
STEP 7 — PAN usually allotted in 2–4 working days, physical card in 15–20 days

🎁 BONUS: Instant e-PAN is FREE on incometax.gov.in — for 18+, Aadhaar-linked mobile, and no existing PAN.

#pancard #pancardapply #nsdl #protean #form93 #epan #incometax #aadhaar #shorts
```
