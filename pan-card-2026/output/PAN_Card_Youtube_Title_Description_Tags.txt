📌 2026 IMPORTANT UPDATES
• New PAN applications by Indian individual citizens now use Form 93 (Form 49A is no longer accepted from 1 April 2026).
• Date of Birth proof: Aadhaar is reported to be no longer accepted as DOB proof – keep a Birth Certificate, Passport or other eligible document ready, and check the current list on the official website.
• Fees (approx., incl. GST): e-PAN ≈ ₹66–₹72, Physical PAN (India) ≈ ₹107. Always check the final amount on the official website.
