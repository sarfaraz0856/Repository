## Notes

- The voice text passes through `scripts/pronunciation.py` before synthesis. English technical words and large numbers are written in Devanagari the way Indian trainers say them. A/B tests with this same Whisper check showed this was clearer than English spelling ("pivot" had been heard as "wire"/"plant", "bars" as "boss", East's total as "…11,030").
- Whisper renders Indian-system amounts with Western commas: "3,051,030" means 3,51,030 (three lakh fifty-one thousand thirty).
- Known residual: in clip 34 Whisper wrote North as "3,055,0133". The same phrase on its own came back as exactly 355,130, so this is most likely the recognizer. It is listed here rather than claimed as perfect.
- Reproduce: `python3 scripts/voice_qa.py` (needs the sherpa-onnx Whisper-small model; paths are at the top of the script).
