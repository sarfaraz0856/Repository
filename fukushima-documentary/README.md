# Fukushima 2011 – Hindi documentary (in production)

This is the next film for Itihaas Ke Saaye, made the same way as the Chernobyl film: 3D animation, captions, score, Hindi narration in the cloned voice, and an English dub.

- [`script/Fukushima-Script-Hindi.md`](script/Fukushima-Script-Hindi.md) holds the cold open and the 12 chapters. Each chapter has its shot list, Hindi narration, on-screen captions and the facts it relies on.
