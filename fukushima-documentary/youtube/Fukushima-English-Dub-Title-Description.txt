2. DESCRIPTION  (English)
--------------
The full story of the Fukushima Daiichi nuclear disaster: the day a magnitude 9.0 earthquake and a 15-metre tsunami cut the power to a nuclear plant on Japan's Pacific coast, and three reactors melted down.

At 2:46 p.m. on March 11, 2011, the biggest earthquake in Japan's recorded history struck under the sea. The reactors shut down safely, but about fifty minutes later the tsunami flooded the emergency generators. With no power to cool the fuel, the cores melted, hydrogen built up, and explosions tore through the reactor buildings. About 154,000 people had to leave their homes.
