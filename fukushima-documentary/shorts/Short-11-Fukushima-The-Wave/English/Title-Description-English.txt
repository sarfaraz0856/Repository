How did the Fukushima nuclear disaster begin? On March 11, 2011, a magnitude 9 earthquake struck Japan. The Fukushima Daiichi plant was designed for a wave of about six metres, but the tsunami that came was 14 to 15 metres high. Sea water flooded the emergency generators, and the pumps that kept the reactors cool stopped. The full Fukushima documentary is on this channel.
