# Surah Al-Fatihah (1) — Horizontal long-form video

**Format:** 1920×1080 horizontal, 30 fps, 69.3 s, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz (loudness −14 LUFS)
Over 60 s and 16:9, so YouTube treats it as a normal video, not a Short. Don't tag it `#shorts`.

**Audio:** the original recitation at natural speed (not sped up), starting at the Bismillah (the ta'awwudh is cut), ending with "Sadaqa Allahu al-Azim". No music or SFX.
**Ayah numbers:** Madinah mushaf numbering, so the Bismillah is ayah 1 and the last ayah is 7.
**Background:** real photo of an open Quran on a rehal in a mosque (Adobe Stock #175296660, free licence, no on-screen credit required), with a very slow push-in. The Quran stays in the upper half and the text sits below it.
**On screen:** Arabic and English together for every ayah, plus a title card at the start and a "share / subscribe" card at the end.
**Fonts:** KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs (ayat), Aref Ruqaa Bold (surah name), Cormorant Garamond SemiBold (English).
